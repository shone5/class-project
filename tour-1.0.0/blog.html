karlovy vary

Karlovy Vary, also known as Carlsbad, is a famous spa town in the western part of the Czech Republic. The city is well known for its beautiful hot springs, elegant architecture, and peaceful atmosphere. It was founded in the 14th century by King Charles IV, and since then it has become one of Europe’s most popular spa destinations. The town is surrounded by forests and hills, giving it a natural, relaxing environment. Visitors come to drink the mineral-rich hot spring water, walk through the colorful colonnades, and enjoy spa treatments. Karlovy Vary is also famous for its international film festival, porcelain (Karlovarský porcelán), and the popular herbal liqueur Becherovka.

Mill Colonnade (Mlýnská kolonáda)

The Mill Colonnade is one of the most iconic landmarks in Karlovy Vary. Built in the 19th century in a grand Neo-Renaissance style, it stretches 132 meters and features 124 majestic columns. It is the largest colonnade in the city and a popular place for visitors to relax and enjoy the spa atmosphere. Inside the colonnade, you will find five different mineral springs, each with its own temperature and healing properties. Visitors can taste the warm mineral water using traditional spa cups while walking through the long, elegant hall.

Diana Observation Tower

The Diana Observation Tower is one of the best viewpoints in Karlovy Vary. Located on a forested hill above the town, the tower offers beautiful panoramic views of the city, surrounding hills, and spa buildings. It was built in 1914 and stands 40 meters tall. Visitors can reach the tower easily by taking the funicular railway from the Grandhotel Pupp area, or they can enjoy a scenic walk through the forest. At the top, you can climb the stairs or take an elevator to the viewing platform.

Karlovy Vary Museum

The Karlovy Vary Museum is a cultural and historical museum that showcases the rich heritage of the city and the entire region. Located near the Teplá River, the museum presents exhibitions about spa traditions, local geology, regional wildlife, and the development of Karlovy Vary over the centuries. Visitors can explore displays of historic photographs, old spa cups, mineral stones, and traditional objects used in the famous healing spa industry. The museum also features temporary exhibitions and educational programs about local culture and nature.

Loket Castle

Loket Castle is a stunning medieval fortress located about 15 minutes from Karlovy Vary. Built in the 12th century, it sits dramatically on a rocky hill almost completely surrounded by the Ohře River, giving it the look of a natural stone “elbow” — which is how the town Loket got its name. The castle is one of the oldest and best-preserved in the Czech Republic. Inside, visitors can explore historic rooms, medieval weapons, torture chambers, and exhibitions about the region’s history. Its tower offers beautiful views of the colorful town below and the surrounding forests.
